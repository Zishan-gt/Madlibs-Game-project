# Madlibs-Game-project

A fun, interactive word game where players fill in blanks with random words (nouns, verbs, adjectives, etc.) without knowing the story context — resulting in a silly, unpredictable story once revealed.

About

Mad Libs is a classic party game invented in the 1950s by Roger Price and Leonard Stern. One player has a story template with key words removed and replaced with labeled blanks (e.g., [noun], [adjective], [verb]). Other players suggest words for each blank without seeing the story, and the words are then plugged in to create a humorous, nonsensical narrative.

How to Play
Choose or load a story template with blank placeholders.
Prompt players for words matching each blank's part of speech.
Insert the words into the story in order.
Read the completed story aloud for laughs.
Features
Multiple story templates
Support for common word types: nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, plural nouns, names, colors, numbers, etc.
Randomized or player-submitted word input
Replayable — same template, different results every time
